Scoring pipeline

Live

Candidate signals are merged by set ID, then scored with configurable non-negative weights:

score = Σ(signal × weight) / Σ(weight)
        over the signals that exist
  • Metadata similarity
  • CLIP visual similarity
  • Personalized classifier probability
  • Catalog fallback (cold start)

Diversity cap and rated-set exclusion are applied after scoring. Weights are configurable in the serving path; no popularity is invented.
